## 5\. Visual Design

### 1\. What overall style will best represent you?

I want my portfolio to have a modern and professional look while still showing some of my creativity\. I don’t want my website to feel crowded, so I want my work to be organized\. Since my background includes technology and creativity I want the website to reflect both sides of my interests\.

### 2\. What color scheme will you use and why?

I want to mix in some tan and white as well as my favorite color light blue\. I think this would give my website a modern professional look without being obnoxious\. The blue will help certain buttons, links, and headings stand out while keeping the design professional\.

### 3\. What fonts will you use for headings and body text?

I would like to use Arial for my headings and body text because it has a simple look and is easy to read\. I’ll use larger, bold text for headings to make each section stand out and keep the body text at a normal size\. I want the font to make my website look professional and easy to read\.

### 4\. How will your design reflect your personality or field?

My design will reflect my interest in technology while also showing my creative side\. Since technology is my main focus, I want to have my website follow more of a professional look\. However, I really enjoy my small business and social media, so I want to highlight that as well\. So while the idea of my business and photos will be creative and colorful, the overall site will need to be modern\.

### 8\. What visual hierarchy will guide visitors?

I want visitors to notice my name and introduction first, followed by my experience and projects\. I’ll use larger headings, bold text, spacing, and different colors to catch their attention\. Important buttons, such as View My Projects and Contact Me, will also stand out\. I may also use animations to keep the website fun but professional\.

### 9\. How will consistency be maintained across pages?

I’ll keep the same colors, fonts, navigation style, buttons, and spacing throughout the website\. Since I’m planning a single\-page portfolio, each section will follow a similar design so everything stays connected\. I’ll also use one main CSS stylesheet to help maintain that consistency\.

### 10\. How will accessibility be considered?

I’ll keep accessibility in mind by choosing readable font sizes, using solid colors and organizing the website with clear headings\. I’ll also include alternative text for images, and make links and buttons easy to see\.

### 11\. Will you use icons, images, or illustrations? Why?

Yes, I plan to include images of my projects, especially my 3D\-printed products and creative work\. I may also use simple icons for my skills, social media links, and contact information\. I want the visuals to make the website fun all while keeping it simple for users to interact with\.

## 6\. Interaction and Functionality

### 11\. What call\-to\-action should visitors take &#40;contact you, view projects, download resume&#41;?

I want visitors to explore my projects, learn more about my experience, and reach out if they’re interested in connecting with me or discussing professional opportunities\. I will structure the website to make it easy for visitors to view my work, download my résumé, and contact me\.

### 1\. What interactive elements will your site include?

My website will include a navigation menu that allows visitors to move between different sections, such as About Me, Experience, Projects, and Contact\. I’ll also include clickable buttons to view my projects, download my résumé, and visit my social media profiles\. I want the website to be professional but simple for users to look at my work\.

### 2\. Will your site include a contact form? How will it work?

Yes, I plan to include a contact form where visitors can enter their name, email address, and a message\. When someone submits the form, the website will check that the required information has been entered correctly\. I have this feature on my 3D printing website so that customers can contact me if they have any questions\. Also, visitors can click on my social media links, and LinkedIn, and contact me from there too\!

### 3\. What JavaScript features will you implement?

I plan to use JavaScript to add basic interactive features to my website\. This will include checking the contact form to make sure the required fields are completed and displaying a message when someone submits it\. I may also use JavaScript for a mobile navigation menu to make the website easier to use on mobile devices\.

### 4\. How will users receive feedback from interactions?

Visitors will receive feedback through visual changes and messages\. For example, buttons and links will change appearance when someone hovers over them, and the contact form will display a message if required information is missing\. After all info is entered, the website will show a confirmation that the information was entered correctly\.

### 5\. How does interactivity improve the user experience?

Interactivity makes my website easier to navigate and more fun for visitors\. Features like clickable navigation links, project buttons, and a contact form will help people find information and interact with my work\. I want visitors to have a good experience and not be confused on where to go\.
